FAQ

Common questions

Is this MP4 to GIF converter really free?
Yes, completely free. There are no hidden fees, no watermarks on the output, and no conversion limits. You can convert as many files as you want.
Is my video uploaded to your servers?
No. All conversion happens directly in your browser using local processing. Your video file never leaves your computer, so your content stays completely private.
What video formats are supported?
The tool supports MP4 (H.264/H.265), MOV (QuickTime), and WEBM files. MP4 is the most widely compatible format and recommended for best results.
How do I reduce the GIF file size?
Lower the frame rate (e.g. 8–10 FPS), choose a smaller output width (e.g. 360px), reduce the quality slider, and trim the clip to only the essential seconds. Each of these adjustments significantly reduces the output file size.
Why does GIF look lower quality than the original video?
GIF is an older format limited to 256 colors per frame, which causes visible dithering compared to modern video codecs. For better-looking animations on the web, consider using MP4 with the autoplay loop muted attributes — it's widely supported and looks much better.
Is there a file size limit?
The tool supports files up to 500MB. For very large files the conversion may take longer depending on your device's performance. Using the clip range to trim the video to only the section you need is recommended for large files.
