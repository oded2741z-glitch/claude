Privacy Policy for ToClipsKit

Effective Date: April 2026

Welcome to ToClipsKit. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and video tools.

1. Local Video Processing (Your Files Remain Yours)

The core functionality of ToClipsKit is completely local. When you use our tools to process a video, the processing happens entirely within your web browser.

  • We do not upload your video files to our servers.
  • We do not store, view, or have access to any media you process using our tools.
  • Your files never leave your device.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

While we do not collect personal data or media files, we use third-party services to keep this tool free and to understand how our website is used. These third parties may collect non-personally identifiable information:

  • Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics to collect basic technical data, including your browser type, device type, approximate geographic location, and how you interact with our website. This data is aggregated and used solely to improve our services.

3. Use of Cookies

A cookie is a small text file saved on your computer or mobile device.

  • Advertising Cookies: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your browsing history.
  • Opting Out: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to request access, deletion, or restriction of the data collected by third parties on our site. Because ToClipsKit itself does not store user accounts or personal data, any requests related to tracking cookies should be managed directly through your browser settings or the third-party opt-out links provided above.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
